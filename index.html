Meet Alfredo: the assistant your business never knew it could have.

Your POS already knows the answers. Alfredo asks the questions. Every week it reads your own transaction data and hands you a short list of moves worth making. Then it measures what actually happened. We do not inflate numbers. We do not estimate around gaps. If the data is not there, the number is not there either.

Our philosophy is simple: no drastic changes. You know your business better than any consultant ever will. Alfredo's job is to hand you the information for small, low cost moves, help you try them, and stack incremental gains that hold up over time.
