The closed loop

Not a dashboard. A weekly to do list that grades itself.

Dashboards show you charts and leave the thinking to you. Alfredo runs a loop: it ranks the moves, you pick one, it measures the outcome, and the result trains the next week's board.

01

Read

Your POS transactions flow in. No new hardware, no staff retraining, no loyalty program required.

02

Rank

The engine scores every candidate move: pricing, cross sell, operations, competitive. You see a short ranked list, not 400 charts.

03

Test

One tap turns a recommendation into a real A/B test on your own menu, with a control item and a time window.

04

Measure

When the window closes, you get a plain verdict: it worked, it did not, or the data was too thin to call.

05

Learn

Every verdict feeds back into the ranking. The board you see in month six knows what your floor already taught it.

The numbers rule

Every number is real, or it is not shown.

Analytics tools earn attention with big projected dollar figures. We think that habit is why operators stopped trusting analytics tools. Alfredo is built fail closed:

No invented figures

Every dollar amount traces back to your own transactions. If we cannot trace it, you do not see it.

Pending means pending

Thin data shows as "needs more data," never as a confident guess dressed up with decimals.

Verdicts, not victory laps

A finished test can say exactly three things: worked, no effect, or backfired. When it backfires, the board says backfired.

No peeking

While a test is running, the dashboard shows no interim results. Checking mid test inflates false positives, so the product does not let anyone peek. Including us.
