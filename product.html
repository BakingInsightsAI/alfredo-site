The closed loop

Read. Rank. Test. Measure. Learn.

Dashboards show you charts and leave the thinking to you. Alfredo runs a loop, and the loop is the product.

01

Read

Your POS transactions flow in. No new hardware, no staff retraining, no loyalty program required.

02

Rank

The engine scores every candidate move: pricing, promotions, positioning, operations. You see a short ranked list, not 400 charts.

03

Test

One tap turns a recommendation into a real A/B test on your own menu, with a control item and a time window.

04

Measure

When the window closes, you get a plain verdict: it worked, it did not, or the data was too thin to call.

05

Learn

Every verdict feeds back into the ranking. The board you see in month six knows what your floor already taught it.

What lands on your board

Four kinds of moves, one ranked list.

Pricing

Which items can carry a price move, which cannot, and a built in test to prove it before you commit the whole menu.

Promotions

Bundles and pairings pulled from your real baskets, with the co order evidence attached. Not your best seller. The pairing you would not have guessed.

Positioning

Where each item sits on your menu and in your market: highlight the hidden gems, dim the underperformers, watch the gaps your competitors leave open.

Operations

Table time, dayparts, happy hour economics, item availability. Where the floor and the data disagree, you find out early.

The menu lab

Your menu is a draft. Treat it like one.

Menu Advisor is a working copy of your menu you can experiment on. Drag items, highlight, dim, badge, reprice, build bundles. Every suggestion arrives with its evidence, and any edit can become a measured test in four clicks.

Suggestions with receipts

Up to ten at a time, each carrying its evidence: co order lift, competitor prices, margin math, verbatim lines from your own reviews.

The test wizard

Pick the item, pick the change, pick a control, pick a duration. The AI proposes control items and shows its match score. You decide.

An honest scoreboard

Your edits tally into estimated impact, and the scoreboard keeps estimates and measured results clearly apart. They never blend.
