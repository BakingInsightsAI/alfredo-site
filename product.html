Product

Three questions Alfredo answers every week.

Alfredo is one assistant with three standing jobs. Each one answers a question you already ask yourself.

What should I do this week, and how do I know it worked?

The weekly board ranks recommendations from your own data, and every one you try comes back with an honest verdict: worked, no effect, or backfired.

What you will find
  • The weekly board: recommendations across pricing, promotions, positioning, and operations, ranked with evidence attached
  • Every trial ends in a verdict: worked, no effect, or backfired
  • A real walkthrough: a 50 cent price change measured week by week, and one change that backfired and rolled back
Explore the weekly board →

Is my menu making me money?

The menu lab lets you test changes safely, and cost intelligence connects what each dish costs to make with what it sells for.

What you will find
  • A working copy of your menu where any edit becomes a measured trial in four clicks
  • Cost intelligence: where each dish's money goes, and where margins quietly leak
  • Drift detection: does your website still match your till?

Who is in my room, and what is happening on my street?

Customer intelligence finds your confirmed guests and regulars from data you already have. Competitive intel watches the venues around you.

What you will find
  • Your confirmed guests and regulars, found in the data you already have
  • Guest segments you can rename in your own words
  • Competitor prices, review sentiment, and the menu scanner
