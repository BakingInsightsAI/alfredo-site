The closed loop

Read. Rank. Try. Measure. Learn.

Dashboards show you charts and leave the thinking to you. Alfredo runs a loop, and the loop is the product.

01

Read

Your POS transactions flow in. No new hardware, no staff retraining, no loyalty program required.

02

Rank

The engine scores every candidate move: pricing, promotions, positioning, operations. You see a short ranked list, not 400 charts.

03

Try

You pick one small change to try: a price, a placement, a pairing nudge. One item at a time, reversible in minutes.

04

Measure

When the window closes, you get a plain verdict: it worked, it did not, or the data was too thin to call.

05

Learn

Every verdict feeds back into the ranking. The board you see in month six knows what your floor already taught it.

Small, careful, and the same menu for everyone.

A trial in Alfredo is one item, one change, for a set number of weeks. Every guest sees the same menu; results are compared against your own history and a control item, never against your customers. If a change backfires, it rolls back in minutes, and the board says so. This is how you were already improving your menu, just measured honestly.

What lands on your board

Four kinds of moves, one ranked list.

Pricing

Which items can carry a price move, which cannot, and a built in way to prove it on one item before you commit the whole menu.

Promotions

Bundles and pairings pulled from your real baskets, with the co order evidence attached. Not your best seller. The pairing you would not have guessed.

Positioning

Where each item sits on your menu and in your market: highlight the hidden gems, dim the underperformers, watch the gaps your competitors leave open.

Operations

Table time, dayparts, happy hour economics, item availability. Where the floor and the data disagree, you find out early.

Customer intelligence

Your regulars, found in your own data.

Alfredo works out who your confirmed guests and regulars are from the data you already have: your loyalty program if you run one, and the transaction data itself if you do not. Either way, no new app to launch and no sign up sheet at the host stand. See who keeps the lights on, how regulars differ from occasional visitors, and which patterns deserve a nudge.

And in keeping with the numbers rule: only confirmed guests are counted. Alfredo never inflates the panel with estimated crowd sizes.
