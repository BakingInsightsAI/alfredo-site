How Alfredo works

It hands you the recommendation, then grades it.

Dashboards show you charts and leave the thinking to you. Alfredo reads your POS, hands you a short ranked list of recommendations worth trying, and when you try one, it measures what actually happened and gets smarter about your business. Suggest, try, verdict, repeat.

No new hardware, nothing to install, no staff retraining. Alfredo connects to the Toast or Clover POS you already run.

Menu trial · completed

Reprice Garlic Fries from $5.50 to $6.50

Worked No effect Backfired

Units fell 22% and net revenue came out $41/wk below baseline. The dollar jump was too big for this item.

Rolled back to $5.50. The engine logged the miss, and next week's pricing suggestions learned from it. A tool that cannot say backfired cannot be trusted when it says worked.

Small, careful, and the same menu for everyone.

A trial in Alfredo is one item, one change, for a set number of weeks. Every guest sees the same menu; results are compared against your own history and a control item, never against your customers. If a change backfires, it rolls back in minutes, and the board says so. This is how you were already improving your menu, just measured honestly.

What lands on your board

Four kinds of recommendations, one ranked list.

Pricing

Which items can carry a price move, which cannot, and a built in way to prove it on one item before you commit the whole menu.

Promotions

Positioning

Where each item sits on your menu and in your market: highlight the hidden gems, dim the underperformers, watch the gaps your competitors leave open.

Operations

Table time, dayparts, happy hour economics, item availability. Where the floor and the data disagree, you find out early.
